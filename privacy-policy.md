# Capy Pomodoro — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 22 November 2026
**Developer:** HIGHEX
**Contact:** apgiiith@gmail.com

Capy Pomodoro is a focus timer with a cosy capybara. The short version: **everything stays on your phone.**
The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It doesn't even ask for permission to use the internet.

## 1. What the app stores (only on your device)

To work, Capy Pomodoro saves a few things in the app's private storage on your phone:

- **Your settings:** timer lengths, theme, sounds, reminders, daily goal and similar.
- **The timer state:** whether a focus session or break is running and when it ends.
- **Your timetable:** the study blocks and routine templates you create.
- **Your focus history:** the start and end times of your sessions, and whether you finished or gave up. The app deletes entries older than about 120 days.
- **Shop progress:** how many strawberries you have, which items you own and which ones Capy is wearing. Strawberries are earned by focusing. They can't be bought with money.

This information never leaves your device. We (the developer) can't see it. It's removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

## 2. Accessibility service ("Capy Focus Guard")

Capy Pomodoro includes an optional Accessibility service called **Capy Focus Guard**. You turn it on yourself in Android's Accessibility settings, after the app explains what it does and you agree.

**What it does:**
- While a focus session you started is running, it notices **which app comes to the front**.
- If that app isn't on the allowed list, it covers the screen with Capy's lock screen.
- The allowed list is: the timer itself, the phone app (including emergency calls), the apps you choose (up to 6 apps in total, WhatsApp by default), your keyboard and the system bars.
- The lock screen has buttons to go back to the timer or open an allowed app.
- Android Settings is never locked: it opens after a one-tap check, so you can always turn the Focus Guard off or uninstall the app. Turning it off during a session ends that session as "given up" inside the app.

**What it does not do:**
- It only listens for "a different window is now in front" events, and it only uses the name of that app.
- It **cannot read** what's on your screen. It doesn't see messages, typing, passwords or any other content.
- It doesn't record which apps you open. It doesn't store them or send them anywhere.
- It does nothing when no focus session is running.

You can turn Capy Focus Guard off at any time in Android's Accessibility settings. The rest of the app keeps working without it.

## 3. Permissions and why they're used

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Shows the running countdown and alerts when a session ends, a break is over, or a timetable block is coming up. |
| Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) | Ends focus sessions and breaks on time, and delivers timetable reminders at the right minute. |
| Run at startup | Puts your timer and reminders back after the phone restarts. |
| Vibration | Lets alerts vibrate. |
| Seeing apps that have a home-screen icon | So you can choose which apps stay open while focusing, and so the Focus Guard can recognise and open them. The list is read on your phone only when you open the app picker. It is never stored (apart from the apps you pick), logged or sent anywhere. |

## 4. Sharing with third parties

None. Capy Pomodoro doesn't share, sell or send any data to anyone. It contains no advertising or analytics software, and no other third-party tracking code.

## 5. Children

Capy Pomodoro is designed for teenagers and adults (13+). It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it doesn't collect personal information at all.

## 6. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. Because nothing is sent over a network, there's nothing to intercept.

## 7. Your choices

- **Delete everything:** Android Settings → Apps → Capy Pomodoro → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.
- **Stop the Focus Guard:** Android Settings → Accessibility → Capy Focus Guard → Off.
- **Turn off notifications:** Android Settings → Apps → Capy Pomodoro → Notifications.
