Capy Pomodoro — Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026 · Developer: HIGHEX · Contact: apgiiith@gmail.com
The short version: everything stays on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no analytics. The app doesn't even ask for permission to use the internet.

1. What the app stores (only on your device)

  • Your settings: timer lengths, theme, sounds, reminders, daily goal and similar.
  • The timer state: whether a session or break is running and when it ends.
  • Your timetable: the study blocks and routine templates you create.
  • Your focus history: session start and end times, and whether you finished or gave up. Entries older than about 120 days are deleted automatically.
  • Shop progress: your strawberries, the items you own and what Capy is wearing. Strawberries are earned by focusing and can't be bought with money.

None of this leaves your device. We can't see it. It's removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall.

2. Accessibility service ("Capy Focus Guard")

This is an optional feature you turn on yourself in Android's Accessibility settings, after the app explains it and you agree. What it does: while a focus session you started is running, it notices which app comes to the front. If that app isn't allowed, it covers the screen with Capy's lock screen. The allowed apps are the timer, phone (including emergency calls), the apps you choose (up to 6 apps in total, WhatsApp by default), your keyboard and the system bars. Android Settings is never locked: it opens after a one-tap check, so you can always turn the Focus Guard off or uninstall the app. Turning it off during a session ends that session as "given up" inside the app.

What it does not do:

  • It only receives "a different window is now in front" events and uses only the app's name.
  • It cannot read your screen. It doesn't see messages, typing, passwords or any other content.
  • It doesn't record, store or send which apps you open.
  • It does nothing when no focus session is running.

You can switch it off at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility → Capy Focus Guard.

4. Sharing with third parties

None. No data is shared, sold or sent anywhere. There's no advertising, analytics or tracking code.

5. Children

Capy Pomodoro is designed for teenagers and adults (13+). It doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

6. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. Nothing is sent over a network.

7. Your choices

  • Delete everything: Settings → Apps → Capy Pomodoro → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall.
  • Stop the Focus Guard: Settings → Accessibility → Capy Focus Guard → Off.
  • Notifications: Settings → Apps → Capy Pomodoro → Notifications.
